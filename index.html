AUGUST 2026

The headline question

Reduced — but from an all-time record

2026 is the second-largest raw cashew crop in history. The decline is real but modest, and every major origin was revised upward as the season progressed.

World RCN production 2026

6.0Mn tonnes
against a 2025 record of 6.2–6.4 Mn t
▼ 3–6% vs 2025

But kernel availability fell further

−6 to −10%
West African outturns dropped 2.0–2.5 lbs per 80 kg bag, so the kernel inside the crop shrank about twice as fast as the crop itself.

Asia · India · Vietnam · Cambodia

Asia's crop went up — and one myth needs killing

India

770–810'000 t
≈ flat · contested
No official 2026 figure exists. DCCD's 2024-25 actual was 802,349 t. The Konkan belt — 37% of the crop — is down 11.1%: Maharashtra −5%, Karnataka −20%, Goa −28%. Do not quote 825,000 t; that is a prior record, not a 2026 estimate.

Vietnam

323'000 t
▲ 4.6%
The "30–50% collapse" is refuted. National output rose. The alarming provincial numbers were mid-season projections for single cooperatives and communes. In any case Vietnam's own crop is only ~10% of what it processes.

Cambodia

0.9–1.0Mn t
▲ exports +10% to +28%
The swing supplier — and the least reliable statistics of any origin. H1 exports of 1.03 Mn t exceed most full-year production estimates. Growth is area-driven: ~120k ha newly bearing. Over 90% leaves raw, to Vietnam.

Where Vietnam's 1.81 Mn t of imported raw nut came from — H1 2026

Cambodia alone supplies more raw nut to Vietnam than Vietnam grows five times over. An El Niño in Cambodia now transmits straight into Vietnamese throughput — an exposure that did not exist in previous cycles.

Also in Asia

  • Indonesia ~150k t, flat. Best KOR reputation in Asia but too small to move the balance — and the only origin with falling farmgate prices in 2026 (−8.6%).
  • Sri Lanka, Myanmar, Thailand, Philippines — minor, no reliable 2026 crop reporting.

Vietnam Ministry of Agriculture & Environment (H1 2026: 323,400 t, +4.6%) · VINACAS · Cambodian customs / CAC · Xinhua · DCCD · MoA second advance estimates via Navhind Times · APEDA

West Africa · the belt from Côte d'Ivoire to The Gambia

Volume held. Quality and policy did not.

Côte d'Ivoire 1,350–1,450 ▼ 6.5–13% 40–47 The single biggest swing in the global balance. Local processors bought 650–700k t vs 500–600k in 2025 — a record kernel year at origin.

Nigeria 350–400 ≈ flat 42→45–50 Good season, priced itself out. Warehouse drying lifted outturn 2–5 lbs. But USD 1,600/t sat ~60% above Côte d'Ivoire — May shipments to Vietnam fell 29%.
Ghana 150 vs 250 ▼ disputed 43–46 Widest official-vs-market gap of any origin (67%). Worst quality in the region — moisture 15–17% against an 8–10% target — while asking the highest farmgate price.
Benin 170–180 ≈ flat 41–47 Best origin-processing gain (~80k t, +30%) and the clearest policy failure: 90–100k t exported illegally despite the ban.

The most actionable finding of the season

Nigerian nuts came in at 42–45 lbs at farmgate and left the warehouse at 45–50 lbs after proper drying. Post-harvest handling — not the tree — sets deliverable quality. Ghana proved the inverse at 15–17% moisture.

ACA AfriCashewSplits 2026 · OFI outturn decline of 2.0–2.5 lbs via Cardassilaris Cashew Market Report, May 2026. ACA: quality "lower than last year's exceptional levels but remains within average levels" — 2025 was an outlier year, so 2026 is reversion, not collapse.

ACA, June 2026: "Costs have risen and RCN stocks are in strong hands. The mismatch between RCN prices and cashew kernels has continued… RCN prices may need to come into line a little."

Outlook · what to watch into 2027

Three risks — and only one of them is weather

01 · El Niño, but on Cambodia

ACA: impact "most likely to be felt in Cambodia and Vietnam". The nuance that matters — "during past events Cambodia was not as important a producer as it is now." At ~1 Mn t and 55% of Vietnam's feedstock, a Cambodian hit now transmits straight into Vietnamese throughput.

Counterweight, presented honestly: the 2023/24 and 2015/16 events had low impact on cashew, and alarmist West African claims were publicly called "incorrect and irresponsible". Real risk, contested magnitude.

02 · Capacity destruction

The risk the tonnage numbers hide entirely. Falling outturn + firm raw nut + easing kernel broke processor economics everywhere at once.

  • Palasa cluster — ~300 units suspended, 17 Apr 2026
  • Andhra's ~700 units at 50–60% capacity
  • Kerala: ~100 active units, from a peak of 830
  • Workforce 300k → ~100k

2027 may open with a smaller global shelling base than 2026 closed with — inflationary for kernels regardless of crop size.

03 · Policy fragmentation

In 2026 alone: Benin's ban leaked 90–100k t · Burkina suspended exports and stranded 180k t · Senegal added a USD 65/t tax · Côte d'Ivoire ran a 10-day processor exclusivity window.

None of it changed how much cashew exists. All of it changed who could get it. India's import book was rewritten in a single season:

  • Togo −84% · Côte d'Ivoire −55%
  • Tanzania to #1 · Mozambique doubled

The take-away for 2027

Model supply by corridor, not by crop — and buy outturn, not tonnage.

Early-season origin estimates in cashew are systematically biased low and get revised up. Anyone who bought the February 2026 panic paid for the correction. Position for that asymmetry in Q1 2027 — and expect KOR-linked contract pricing to spread fast after a season like this one.
